CardFaceSpriteHolder read-only inspector

New per-popup selector:
  /sdcard/Android/data/com.ea.gp.pvzheroes/files/pick3_holder_inspect.txt
  0 = disabled (default)
  1 = log holder runtime class, CardFaceSprites field/list availability,
      list Count and whether the candidate GUID falls inside that Count.

Recommended settings (experimental direct loader must already work):
  pick3_holder_inspect.txt=1
  pick3_direct_loader.txt=1
  pick3_async_art.txt=1
  pick3_async_override.txt=1
  pick3_art_trace.txt=2
  pick3_resident_art.txt=0

The inspector does NOT enumerate individual sprites, change GetSprite lookup,
call additional asset loads, or weaken the loader type guard.

Read logs:
  adb logcat -d -v time -s Sprig:I "*:S" | Select-String "HOLDER_INSPECT|CALLBACK_RESULT" | Select-Object -Last 80

Disable experimental loading if crashes occur.
